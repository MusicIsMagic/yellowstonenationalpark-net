Sights and hikes in Yellowstone

The big names, what each is like, and how much walking it takes.

Check conditions first. Road closures, trail closures, bear activity, and thermal-area rules change often. Figures here are approximate. Confirm current details at nps.gov/yell.

Old Faithful and the Upper Geyser Basin

Best time
Morning or evening
Walk
1 to 3 mi of boardwalk
Effort
Easy

Old Faithful erupts about every 90 minutes, and the visitor center posts predicted times. Boardwalks continue past Castle, Grand, and other geysers. Come early or late to avoid the crowds.

Grand Prismatic Spring

Best time
Midday, in full sun
Walk
0.8 mi boardwalk
Effort
Easy

The colors come from heat-loving microbes that change with the water's temperature. A short overlook trail off the Fairy Falls route gives the best view of the whole ring from above.

Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone

Best time
Morning light
Walk
short viewpoint walks
Effort
Easy to moderate

Yellow and orange canyon walls frame the 308-foot Lower Falls. The North and South Rim drives offer many viewpoints, and Artist Point is among the best.

Lamar Valley

Best time
Dawn and dusk
Walk
roadside viewing
Effort
Easy

Often called America's Serengeti for its herds of bison and its wolves and bears. Pull off safely, stay in your car near animals, and bring binoculars or a spotting scope.

Mammoth Hot Springs

Best time
Any season
Walk
1 to 2 mi boardwalk
Effort
Easy to moderate

Flowing water has built white terraces of travertine. The formations change from year to year, and some terraces dry out. It sits near the park's north entrance and its year-round road.

Yellowstone Lake

Best time
Summer
Walk
shoreline walks
Effort
Easy

One of the largest high-elevation lakes in North America. The water stays very cold, so boating and swimming carry real risk. West Thumb Geyser Basin sits on the shore.

Hikes worth planning around

Most of the park is above 7,000 feet, so even short climbs feel harder. Carry bear spray where trail rules recommend it, hike in groups, and make noise on the trail.

Mount Washburn

Round trip
about 6 mi
Climb
about 1,400 ft
Effort
Moderate

A wide trail climbs to a lookout tower with a view over much of the park. Bighorn sheep and wildflowers are common, and snow can linger into early summer.

Fairy Falls

Round trip
about 5 mi
Climb
about 200 ft
Effort
Easy

A flat trail through forest to a 200-foot waterfall, with a short side trail up to the Grand Prismatic overlook.
