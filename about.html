About this guide

YellowstoneNationalPark.net is an independent visitor guide. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the National Park Service, the U.S. Department of the Interior, or any other government agency.

Official information

Yellowstone National Park is a unit of the National Park Service. Road status, alerts, permits, fees, and maps come from nps.gov/yell. We link there whenever a rule matters.

What this site does

We explain the sights, seasons, and logistics that shape a trip to Yellowstone, and we help visitors find lodging, guides, gear, and transportation. When you send a request through the trip planning form, it may be shared with providers who can help.

Accuracy

Information was last reviewed in October 2026. Park conditions, road dates, trail access, permit systems, and fees change, so treat numbers here as approximate and confirm them with the National Park Service before you travel.
